CUSTOMER RETENTION & CHURN ANALYSIS
Future Interns - Data Science Internship
Task 2

1. PROJECT OBJECTIVE

The objective of this project is to analyze customer churn patterns,
identify important retention drivers, understand customer lifetime
trends, and provide business recommendations to reduce customer loss.

2. DATASET

The analysis uses a simulated customer subscription dataset containing
customer demographic, subscription, usage, support, satisfaction,
lifetime value, and churn information.

Total Customers: 1500

3. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS

The analysis measures:

- Total Customers
- Churned Customers
- Retained Customers
- Churn Rate
- Retention Rate
- Average Customer Tenure
- Average Monthly Charge
- Average Customer Lifetime Value
- Average Satisfaction Score

4. CHURN ANALYSIS

Customer churn was analyzed across:

- Contract Type
- Subscription Plan
- Region
- Tenure Group
- Churn Reason

These comparisons help identify customer groups with higher churn risk.

5. CUSTOMER LIFETIME ANALYSIS

Customer Lifetime Value was compared across churn status and tenure
groups to understand the relationship between customer retention,
tenure, and long-term customer value.

6. CUSTOMER RISK ANALYSIS

Customers were categorized into:

- High Risk
- Medium Risk
- Low Risk

The risk segmentation considers contract type, satisfaction score,
support calls, tenure, and subscription plan.

7. RETENTION RECOMMENDATIONS

Based on the analysis, the following actions are recommended:

- Focus retention campaigns on high-risk customers.
- Improve customer satisfaction and service quality.
- Monitor customers with frequent support calls.
- Address the major reasons responsible for customer churn.
- Provide targeted offers to customers using high-churn plans.
- Encourage customers to move toward longer-term contracts.
- Give special attention to customers with low tenure and low
  satisfaction.

8. CONCLUSION

The analysis demonstrates how customer data can be used to identify
churn patterns and potential retention opportunities.

The combination of churn analysis, customer lifetime value analysis,
and risk segmentation can help businesses take proactive actions to
reduce customer loss and improve long-term customer relationships.
